title=Cooler Fridge
description=Coolers finally do what coolers are for.[br][br]
description=Pack a cooler with ice and the food inside it keeps four times as long, anywhere you carry it - on foot, in a car, out in the woods. No power, no fridge, no base required.[br][br]
description=[h1]What it adds[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Bag of Ice[/b] - a new item. Loot it from shop and household freezers, or make your own.
description=[*][b]Iced coolers preserve food[/b] - drop ice in any cooler (including the Beer, Meat, Soda and Seafood coolers) and everything inside rots at a quarter of the normal rate. The cooler is labelled [i](Iced)[/i] while it is working, and the chilled items turn blue in the inventory window exactly like food in a working fridge.
description=[*][b]Ice melts[/b] - about two days inside a cooler, five times faster loose in a backpack, and faster still in a Kentucky summer. The bag shows how much is left.
description=[*][b]Make your own ice[/b] - right-click a container of water inside a powered fridge or freezer, choose [i]Freeze Into Ice[/i], and leave it there for six hours. Melted bags left in a powered freezer refill themselves.
description=[*][b]Cold Packs work too[/b], at 40% strength, if you leave the option on.
description=[/list][br]
description=[h1]Sandbox options[/h1]
description=Cooling strength, ice lifetime, melt rate outside a cooler, freezing time, water per bag, cold pack support and freezer loot are all configurable under the [b]Cooler Fridge[/b] sandbox page.[br][br]
description=[h1]Notes[/h1]
description=Build 42 only. Safe to add to an existing save. Multiplayer friendly - it runs client side on the containers you are actually carrying or looking at, so a dedicated server needs nothing beyond the mod being installed.[br][br]
description=Other mods can register their own coolers and cold sources by adding to [i]CoolerFridge.CoolerBags[/i] and [i]CoolerFridge.IceSources[/i].
