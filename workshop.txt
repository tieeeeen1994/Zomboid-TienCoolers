title=Tien's Coolers
description=A cooler packed with ice keeps the food inside it fresh four times longer, anywhere you carry it. No power and no refrigerator are required.[br][br]
description=Coolers already exist in Project Zomboid, but they do nothing for the food they hold. This mod gives them their intended purpose and adds the ice to fill them with.[br][br]
description=[h1]What the mod adds[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Bag of Ice.[/b] A new item. It is found in shop display freezers and in household and garage chest freezers, and it can also be made at home.
description=[*][b]Cooling.[/b] Put a bag of ice in any cooler, including the Beer, Meat, Soda and Seafood coolers, and the food inside rots at a quarter of the usual rate. The cooler is labelled [i](Iced)[/i] for as long as the ice lasts, and the chilled items are tinted blue in the inventory window in the same way as food in a working refrigerator.
description=[*][b]Melting.[/b] A full bag lasts about two days inside a cooler and melts roughly five times faster outside one. Hot weather shortens it further. How much ice is left is shown on the bag itself.
description=[*][b]Freezing water.[/b] Right-click a container of water inside a powered refrigerator or freezer and choose [i]Freeze Into Ice[/i]. Six hours later the water has become a bag of ice. A bag that has melted refills itself if it is left in a powered freezer.
description=[*][b]Cold Packs.[/b] The vanilla Cold Pack chills a cooler as well, at 40 percent of the strength of a bag of ice.
description=[/list][br]
description=[h1]Sandbox options[/h1]
description=Nine settings are available on the [b]Tien's Coolers[/b] sandbox page: cooling strength, ice lifetime, melting speed outside a cooler, freezing time, the water needed per bag, cold pack support, freezer loot, the blue tint on chilled items and the [i](Iced)[/i] label.[br][br]
description=The mod also follows the vanilla Food Rot Speed and Refrigeration Effectiveness settings, and a cooler is never allowed to preserve food better than a powered refrigerator does.[br][br]
description=[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
description=Build 42 only. The mod can be added to an existing save. Multiplayer is supported, and a dedicated server needs no additional setup beyond having the mod installed.[br][br]
description=Other mods can register their own coolers and cold sources. The readme explains how.
