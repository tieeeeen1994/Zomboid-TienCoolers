title=Tien's Coolers
description=[h2]New in 1.5.*: Reworked Ice, a beta option, off by default[/h2]
description=Version 1.5 adds a beta sandbox option, [b]Reworked Ice[/b], which makes ice behave like the water it is made of. It is [b]off by default[/b], so the mod plays exactly as it did before until it is switched on in the sandbox settings, where it is marked [BETA]. With it on:
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Tainted ice:[/b] tainted water freezes into a Bag of Ice (Tainted), which cools food just as well but melts into tainted water. A clean bag topped up with tainted water becomes tainted.
description=[*][b]Plastic bags:[/b] each new bag of ice made in a freezer needs an empty plastic bag or garbage bag, which is given back when the ice is used up.
description=[*][b]Catch meltwater:[/b] right-click a bottle, pot or other water container inside a cooler and choose [i]Catch Meltwater[/i], and the water from the melting ice runs into it until it is full. Water that melts while nothing is set to catch it is lost.
description=[*][b]Freezing takes water:[/b] water set to freeze turns to ice a little at a time over the freezing time, filling one bag of ice before starting the next, and water short of a full bag makes a partly filled one. A melted bag fills up again only from water set to freeze beside it.
description=[*][b]Weight:[/b] a bag of ice weighs as much as the water in it and gets lighter as it melts.
description=[/list]

description=A cooler packed with ice preserves the food inside it half as well as a working refrigerator, anywhere you carry it, with no power required.

description=Put a Bag of Ice in any cooler, including the Beer, Meat, Soda and Seafood coolers, and the food inside rots more slowly. The cooler is labelled [i](Iced)[/i] while the ice lasts, and chilled items are tinted blue.
description=
description=A full bag lasts about two days inside a cooler, melts much faster outside one and runs out sooner in hot weather. A melted bag refreezes in a powered freezer.
description=
description=Bags of Ice can be found in freezers. To make your own, right-click water in any powered fridge or freezer, chest freezers included, and choose [i]Freeze Into Ice[/i]. Water set to freeze in the same container adds up, and a bag is ready after seven hours.

Cooling follows the vanilla Food Rot Speed and Refrigeration Effectiveness settings and never exceeds a powered refrigerator.
