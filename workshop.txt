description=[h2]New in 1.5.*: beta features, off by default[/h2]
description=Version 1.5 adds three new features as a beta. All of them are [b]off by default[/b], so the mod plays exactly as it did before until they are switched on in the sandbox settings, where each one is marked [BETA].
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Tainted Ice:[/b] tainted water freezes into a Bag of Ice (Tainted), which cools food just as well but melts into tainted water.
description=[*][b]Freezing Needs Plastic Bags:[/b] each bag of ice made in a freezer needs an empty plastic bag or garbage bag, which is given back when the ice is used up.
description=[*][b]Catch Meltwater:[/b] right-click a bottle, pot or other water container inside a cooler and choose [i]Catch Meltwater[/i], and the water from the melting ice runs into it until it is full. Water that melts while nothing is set to catch it is lost, and water that has run out of a bag cannot be frozen back into it.
description=[/list]
description=
description=Please report any bugs if you found one. Thank you!
description=
description=A cooler packed with ice preserves the food inside it half as well as a working refrigerator, anywhere you carry it, with no power required.
description=
description=Put a Bag of Ice in any cooler, including the Beer, Meat, Soda and Seafood coolers, and the food inside rots more slowly. The cooler is labelled [i](Iced)[/i] while the ice lasts, and chilled items are tinted blue.
description=
description=A full bag lasts about two days inside a cooler, melts much faster outside one and runs out sooner in hot weather. A melted bag refreezes in a powered freezer.
description=
description=Bags of Ice can be found in freezers. To make your own, right-click water in any powered fridge or freezer, chest freezers included, and choose [i]Freeze Into Ice[/i]. Water set to freeze in the same container adds up, and a bag is ready after seven hours.
description=
description=The vanilla Cold Pack also chills a cooler, at a lower strength than ice.
description=
description=Thirteen sandbox settings cover cooling strength, ice lifetime, melting, freezing, cold packs, freezer loot, the blue tint, the [i](Iced)[/i] label and the three beta features. Cooling follows the vanilla Food Rot Speed and Refrigeration Effectiveness settings and never exceeds a powered refrigerator.
